Give your spare phone
a new view.

One device is the camera. The other is your viewer. See live video, choose when to listen and keep original recordings on the camera device.

Free App Store release in preparation · Not yet released

Connect over your home network

Find nearby cameras automatically. Compare pairing numbers or scan a QR code. No account, typed IP address or password. Media and controls are encrypted between paired devices.

Smooth preview. Original recordings.

Preview at up to 720p while recording at the camera’s selected native format, up to 4K on supported hardware. Send recording commands from the viewer, then download original clips for playback.

Sound is your choice

The camera microphone, automatic listening and background listening all default to off. Frame timestamps, dark appearance, and Picture in Picture and Live Activities on compatible devices help you keep track.

What you need

Two devices running iOS / iPadOS 17 or later on the same reachable local network. Keep the camera app open in the foreground; its black view is not the device lock screen. Viewing outside your local network and automatic crying or incident alerts are not included.

Simplified Chinese and English. Quality, frame rates, lenses and system features depend on your devices. Use only where you have the necessary permission and consent.
