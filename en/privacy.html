Privacy policy

LanternCam · Updated October 27, 2026

This policy describes information handling in LanternCam and this website. The developer is Qinglin Liu. Contact: hvnt3r@foxmail.com.

Local video, audio and controls

The device you choose as a camera captures video and streams a preview to a paired viewer. Audio is captured, transmitted and recorded only after you enable the camera microphone. Media and control messages are encrypted between paired devices over your local network, without a developer-operated cloud relay.

We do not receive or upload your camera images, audio, recordings, pairing keys or app usage analytics to developer-operated servers. The app has no account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or cross-app tracking.

System permissions

  • Camera: capture video on the camera device, or scan a pairing code on the viewer.
  • Microphone: used only when enabled on the camera device; off by default.
  • Local Network: discover, pair with and stream media between devices.

Manage permissions in system Settings. Denying a permission prevents the associated feature from working. The app does not request contacts or location access.

Information kept on your devices

Recordings stay in the camera app’s local directory. Original clips and incomplete downloads on a viewer stay on that viewer until you remove the corresponding data. Recording policies, display and audio preferences, recording indexes and limited device-operation events are stored locally. Pairing keys are held in the system Keychain.

The app does not add file-level encryption to saved recordings. Device protection and device backups are managed by iOS / iPadOS. App files may be included in system or iCloud device backups depending on your settings. Copies you export to Photos, Files or another app are managed by the destination you choose.

Optional status and background features

Paired devices may exchange battery, charging, thermal and capture status. Storage-capacity sharing is off by default. Automatic listening and background listening on the viewer are off by default. If enabled, real microphone audio may continue for as long as the system permits. Live Activities and Dynamic Island show minimal viewing/recording status, without pairing secrets; this status may be visible on the Lock Screen.

Retention, removal and pairing reset

Loop cleanup is off by default. Choose recording segment length, quota, retention days and whether to enable loop recording. Protected clips and files currently being transferred are excluded from loop cleanup. Remove files in the local recording library, or reset pairing on the camera to invalidate previous pairing. Uninstalling normally removes the app directory, but Keychain entries, system backups and exported copies may remain. Reset pairing first if you need to revoke device access.

Diagnostics and contacting support

The app contains no third-party diagnostic upload service. Apple may process and provide developers with crashes, testing feedback or diagnostics according to your system or TestFlight settings and Apple’s privacy policy. If you email support, we process the address and content you choose to send to answer your request. Please do not send pairing codes, keys or private camera footage.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages with no added analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub may process IP addresses and request logs to run and protect its service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Use and updates

Only capture or transmit images and sound you have the right to process, with the required consent. The app is not a guaranteed continuous security alert or medical monitoring system. Updates to this policy will carry a new date on this page. Contact the address above for questions or support-related removal requests.
