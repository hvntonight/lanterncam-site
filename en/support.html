Support

Connect your devices, then start viewing.

First connection

  1. Install the same latest LanternCam version on both devices and connect to the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Choose “Use as camera” on your spare device. Allow Camera and Local Network access, then keep the app open.
  3. Choose “Monitor” on the other device. Open “Pair nearby”, compare the numbers on both devices, or scan the camera’s pairing QR code.

Camera not found or connection failed

Allow Local Network access on both devices. Guest Wi-Fi, AP isolation, VPNs or router isolation may prevent discovery or connections. QR pairing also requires a reachable local network. Resetting pairing requires pairing again. Do not share pairing codes.

No sound

Stop recording on the camera device, enable its microphone and grant microphone permission. Then tap “Listen” on the viewer. Check system media volume and Bluetooth output. Automatic and background listening can be chosen separately and default to off. Automatic crying alerts are not included.

Where recordings are saved, and why preview is 720p

Clips are saved on the camera device; the viewer sends recording commands. A smaller preview reduces network load without changing the selected original recording format. Resolutions, frame rates and lenses come from the camera’s hardware. Playback downloads the original clip first, and an interrupted download can be resumed.

Black screen or background interruption

The camera controls hide after one minute; tap five times to restore them. Do not lock the camera or send the app to the background, where iOS may stop capture. The viewer can use Picture in Picture on supported devices or opt-in background listening. System interruptions, network changes, closing the app or stopping PiP may end monitoring.
